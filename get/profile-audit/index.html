Questions

Before you paste your link.

Is it really free?

Yes. No call and no card. Your page has a link to book a call if you want me to run it for you, and that is the only ask.

What do you look at?

Only what is public: your profile, your recent posts, the people engaging with them, and your site. I never log into your account, and nothing gets posted or sent in your name.

How long does it take?

Usually a few hours. It arrives by email as a link to your own page.

Why is there a daily limit?

Every page is built one by one, so I cap it at 10 a day. If today is full, the form tells you and you can try again tomorrow.

Who is it for?

Agency owners and founders who sell through LinkedIn and want more of the people reading their posts to book a call.
