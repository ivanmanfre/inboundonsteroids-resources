Five records to add on risedtc.com.

For whoever manages the risedtc.com DNS. Add these five, leave everything else exactly as it is. Your existing email and website records do not change. It takes about five minutes and nothing on the live site is affected.

The first record puts the lead magnets on resources.risedtc.com. The next three let the newsletter send as mattanupdates@risedtc.com, and the last one tells inboxes the domain signs its mail, which helps it land in the inbox. On GoDaddy the Host is just the short part shown, it adds .risedtc.com for you. Some panels want the full name (for example send.risedtc.com), either form is fine.

Two things to know

Add, do not replace. Leave the existing MX and any current records in place. These five are additions. Reader replies are set to go to mattan@risedtc.com, nothing to configure for that.
