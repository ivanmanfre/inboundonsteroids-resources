06 / Straight talk

The seat risk, stated plainly

Here is the honest version of how the cold outreach part works, so there are no surprises. If you are good with it, sign at the bottom.

Where cold outreach runs

Your cold outreach runs on your own LinkedIn and Sales Navigator seat, through a secure connection. It goes out under your name, because that is what makes it land with founders. We operate it for you day to day.

The real risk, stated plainly

LinkedIn limits and sometimes restricts accounts based on outreach volume. This is true for every account that does outreach, no matter who runs it or how careful they are. It is a platform rule, not a reflection of quality. So there is always some risk when an account sends connection requests and messages at any real volume.

How we keep that risk low

We warm your seat up slowly over the first two weeks at low volume before reaching a steady pace. We hold that steady pace well under LinkedIn's limits on purpose, rather than pushing for maximum volume. We watch the account closely, and if LinkedIn shows any warning at all, we pause outreach on your seat immediately.

What happens if your account does get restricted

We stop all outreach on your seat right away. We wait out the restriction, let the account rest, then resume at a lower, slower volume to rebuild trust. Your content, your captured leads, and your inbound engine keep running the whole time, because they do not depend on the outreach seat.

How to think about cold

Cold outreach is additional upside on top of your inbound content base. It is not a guaranteed number of calls or deals. It is a channel we run carefully to create more conversations over time, alongside the content that compounds on its own.

Acknowledgment

By signing, you acknowledge that outreach runs on your LinkedIn and Sales Navigator seat, that LinkedIn restrictions are a real platform risk that no operator can fully remove, and that you accept that risk in exchange for the added reach it creates.
