Questions.

What do you look at?

Public data only: your public profile, your public posts and the people who publicly engaged with them. We never ask for your LinkedIn login or password, and nothing gets posted or sent in your name.

How long does it take?

If we already cover your niche (AI and automation, DTC ecommerce, mobile games and apps), usually minutes. For a new niche we first pull a fresh set of peers, which takes about 2 hours. Either way it arrives by email.

Who am I compared with?

Creators in your niche with about your follower count. Your report names them and shows where each one sits, so you can see the comparison is fair.

Why only posts at least a week old?

A post keeps collecting likes and comments for days. Comparing posts once they are at least a week old keeps the ranking fair for everyone in it.

What if I have only posted a few times?

Your rank and patterns come from your own posts. Where a finding rests on too few posts, the report marks it as too few to call.

What lands in my inbox?

A link to your private report page, a PDF of the full report, and 3 carousel PDFs you can upload to LinkedIn as they are.

How does the free re-scan work?

Your report shows your re-scan date, 30 days out. We run the same scan again then and show your before and after numbers, at no extra cost.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay $99 once and nothing renews.

What if my report misses the mark?

If the report does not have your rank and plan, reply to the email and you get your money back.

I lost the email. Now what?

Ask for your report link again and we send it to the email you ordered with.
