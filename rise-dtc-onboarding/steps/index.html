Every reply on his seat runs through one router, so he is never on the hook to babysit cold DMs:

  • "Stop", annoyed, not interested → the sequence halts on its own and they drop out. No second message after a no.

  • Anything we cannot answer → flagged into your shared Slack for a human to take.

Auto-replies and out-of-office get ignored, not counted as engagement. Wrong-person replies stop the sequence.
