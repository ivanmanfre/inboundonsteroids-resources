02 • The Solution

What we’re building

Swipe to explore the full flow →

Once it's running, the daily lift is under 10 minutes. Posts queue up in Notion each week, drafted in your voice, grounded in your client calls and case-study back catalog. Carson reads the draft, taps approve, done. LinkedIn schedules itself. Lead magnets branch automatically from approved topics. No briefing, no writing, nothing to manage.

Six formats, applied to your audience

Every approved post topic branches into one of six lead-magnet formats. The system picks the one that fits the topic so the library stays varied month over month. Each asset ships with a branded landing page, resource page, cover image, and ConvertKit email. More formats get built into the engine as the partnership continues.
