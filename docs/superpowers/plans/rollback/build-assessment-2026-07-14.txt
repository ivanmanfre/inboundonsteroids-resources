### Hard rules (enforce on every emission)

1. **Zero em dashes.** Use hyphens, colons, commas, or sentence breaks.
2. **No Corrective Contrast.** Banned forms: "The fix isn't X. It's Y." / "That's not a \[X\]. That's a \[Y\]." / "You think it's X. It's actually Y." / "X — not Y" / "X, not Y". If a contrast is genuinely essential, max 1 per piece, never as opener, requires ≥2 sentences of setup, and never repeated.
3. **No "Not just X, but Y"** in any form. Wikipedia-flagged top AI tell.
4. **No "stop X start Y" / "less X more Y" / "we don't X we Y" / "more than just X" / "instead of just X"** — Corrective Contrast siblings, all banned by extension.
5. **No round-number listicle openers**: "5 ways to...", "7 secrets of...", "12 lessons from...", "X laws of...", "9 reasons why...".
6. **No banned closers**: "What are your thoughts?", "Thoughts?", "Tag a founder who needs this", "Save this for later", "Bookmark this", "Hope this helps", "Let me know if you need anything".
7. **No banned filler words**: delve, leverage, harness, seamless, robust, holistic, empower, elevate, utilize, unlock, unpack, streamline, cutting-edge, game-changer, transformative, ecosystem (as biz buzzword), AI-powered, disrupting, deep dive, learnings, stakeholder (as filler), supercharge, "rockstar", "crushing it", "purpose-driven", boasts, "stands as", "serves as", "represents", "embodies". Full list in Forbidden Language §1.
8. **No formulaic transitions**: "But here's the bottleneck", "Here's what most people miss", "This is where it gets interesting", "Here's the kicker", "And that's the point", "Here's the thing". Replace with no transition, or "anyway", "point is", "which means", "look".
9. **No three-imperative cadence closers** ("Ship it. Run it. Measure where it broke.").
10. **No three-item dramatic lists** ("Every campaign. Every test. Every metric.").
11. **No stacked declarations** ("That's the game. That's the truth. Full stop.").
12. **No anonymized-client launder** ("A 47-person legal firm called me last month..."). Use Kyle-style `$XXk/mo [vertical] [role]` identifier instead ("a $72k/mo agency owner").

### Preferred (DO use)

*   Active voice, concrete nouns, short strong verbs: **build / ship / shipped / built / run**.
*   Quantified two-state outcomes with a time window: "$40k → $130k/mo in 90 days".
*   `→` arrow lists for pain points (≤12 words per item; mix with prose).
*   Direct quoted client lines (loosely attributed).
*   Buyer-register over consultant-register (see Forbidden Language §9 translation table).
*   Human markers (PERMITTED): doubt, frustration, unfinished thought, register shift ("yeah" / "anyway" / "tbh"), approximation over precision, self-correction, ellipsis (`...`) as breath/pause (~1 per piece, NOT em-dash substitute).
*   Identity verbs: **builder, not consultant/advisor/strategist**.
