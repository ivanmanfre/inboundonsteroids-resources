# Stochastic Research

Research a question from several angles at once, verify, and synthesize.

The question: $ARGUMENTS

1. Spawn 4-5 agents in parallel (Task tool). Each takes a DIFFERENT angle — e.g. how-to, costs/tradeoffs, who has done it, what goes wrong, alternatives. Tell each to ground findings in real sources (web search) and in your setup (read CLAUDE.md) — not generic advice.
2. Adversarial check: spawn a skeptic to challenge the shakiest claims; drop anything that can't be backed up.
3. Synthesize a short report: the answer, the evidence, the tradeoffs, and a recommended next step. Cite sources.
