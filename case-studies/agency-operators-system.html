Questions that come up most.

Will it actually sound like me?

Yes, once it has enough of your voice to learn from. The profile trains off your LinkedIn archive, your call transcripts, and a short brand interview in week one. After 10–15 approved posts the edit time drops to seconds per draft.

What do you need from me to build it?

A 30-minute brand interview in week one. Access to your LinkedIn archive. Your call recordings if you have them (Fathom, Granola, whatever). Approval rights on the accounts where the system lives. That's the list.

Can I edit drafts before they ship?

Every one. The queue shows the draft, the ten hook variations, the source material, and the QA score. Edit, reject, swap hooks, change publish time. Nothing goes live without your tap.

Am I locked in after the build?

No. The system runs in your accounts on your stack. Database, workspace, LinkedIn integration, landing pages, all in your name. Optional care plan if you want me to monitor and tune. Most clients keep me on it. A few run it themselves from day 15. Your call.

What if the output isn't good enough?

Weekly reviews during the build catch direction problems early. If at the end of the engagement the system isn't producing usable output, I refund the final invoice. Hasn't happened yet.
